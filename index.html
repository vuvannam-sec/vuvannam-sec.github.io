Research index

Two findings.
Two trust boundaries.

Reported to the vendors, fixed, and written up with the details that mattered.

01Genspark AI ChatWhen generated output was rendered as executable HTML.Stored XSS+

The boundary

AI-generated content was treated as trusted markup. The stored payload executed when someone opened a shared conversation.

The evidence

Script execution, persistence and execution for a second viewer were observed. Automatic propagation is not established by that PoC.

Read the disclosure ↗
02Cốc Cốc extensionWhen selected text became a browser execution path.DOM-based XSS+

The boundary

The Dictionary & Translate extension inserted selected text into the DOM as HTML instead of plain text.

The outcome

The issue was reported with a proof of concept and subsequently fixed. The write-up documents the behavior and disclosure timeline.
